## Tooltips and glossary (in More → Start here)

- **Best bet:** an official Kook'n play, counted in the public record at one unit at the price and book we posted.
- **Research:** lines we track and grade but didn't post.
- **Unit (u):** one standard stake.
- **Break-even:** how often a bet must win to not lose money at that price (−110 needs 52.4%).
- **Edge:** our chance minus the break-even chance, in percentage points.
- **Fair price:** the odds that match our chance exactly.
- **Closing line:** the final number before kickoff. Beating it more often than not is the best early sign of skill.
- **Fun ticket:** a longshot parlay at a smaller stake, tracked separately.
- **80/20 Climb:** $50 to $1,000. Bank 20% of each win, ride 80%.
