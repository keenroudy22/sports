## Deep review: Harry Lock Picks, Oct 7

Read-only pass over @HarryLockPicks: 77 original posts, Sep 30 to Oct 7, taken from the public fxtwitter mirror. No X
login was used, and nothing was clicked, liked or followed. Metrics were captured at different post ages. This is a
creative benchmark, not proof of what works for a 77-follower account.

**Who it is:**
- 255.7K followers, following 137, about 30K posts, joined Oct 2022.
- The bio says "Sports betting insider from the UK, sharing free NFL, Soccer & UFC picks daily."
- The location field reads "Get a GameScript trial", and X lists the account's verification type as organization. Dan's AI sells the same GameScript app. These person-led accounts are part of a paid-app funnel.
- **Avatar:** an illustrated cartoon of a man cheering with a fist pump, wearing a green polo and a gold watch, on a green background. A face, not a logo.
- **Banner:** a real photo of a sportsbook bar.

**Volume:** about 11 posts a day. Daily counts were 4, 14, 10, 7, 21 (Sunday), 14, 6 and 1. About a third are live reactions or jokes during games.

### What each format earns

- **Saves** go to tickets with a clear hook (boosted, 100% hit rate, every RedZone game), the predictions video and the early-line list.
- **Likes without saves** go to wins (💥 BOOOOM, "Smash ❤️ for a +473 winner": 177 likes / 3 saves) and live reactions (PRIMETIME BIJAN!! video: 136 likes).
- **Personal life works as a lead-in.** "I've got some news guys... heading to the Patriots @ Bills game tomorrow" drew 110K views and 214 likes but 0 saves. The SGP drop that followed drew 194K / 340 / 155. He then posted stadium photos and the Niagara weather.
- **The weakest posts:** GameScript VIP pitches (11 to 45 likes, 0 to 8 saves), the giveaway (21 likes) and "last chance to tail" link hubs (36 to 52 likes).
- **Data sheets do fine but not great:** receptions and targets leaders 40 saves, "hit this line every game" list 63, receiving-room comparison 33.

### Voice

- He sounds like a person watching with you. First person, jokes and callbacks ("Has anyone seen Dalton Kincaid? 😭").
- He names the bad beats ("🪝 nasty hooks today").
- When the challenge loses, he says it plainly: "The french stop us climbing the mountain… The Money Mountain will return."
- He asks for disagreement: "Let me know what I've got wrong 👇🏼".
- He has no W-L record, no units and no misses on a card. Losses live only in a text line.
