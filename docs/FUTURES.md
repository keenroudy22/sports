# Season futures

Added to the product plan October 3, 2026. Futures are a separate preseason research track, not extra daily-card
plays. No futures selection is live merely because this plan exists.

## What belongs here

- Championship, conference and division winners.
- Make/miss playoffs and other season-qualification markets.
- Regular-season win totals or their sport-specific equivalent.
- Awards only after player eligibility, availability and settlement coverage are reliable for that sport.

Each league gets its own opening window as it is added to Kook'n. A calendar date is only a reminder to look; the
desk records a market only when an exact price is actually available from an approved source.

## The record

Every watched or published future must preserve the league, season, exact market, selection or threshold, direction,
American price, sportsbook, jurisdiction, source, observation time and the model/research snapshot used at that
time. The first observed quote is immutable. Later price moves append dated observations; they never replace the
opening number.

Research watches stay separate from official futures. An official future also records its publication time, stake
in units and final book settlement. Missing prices produce no ROI. A void, dead heat, shortened season, format
change, player ineligibility or other special result is not guessed from a standings page; use the listed book's
documented settlement when it is available, otherwise leave the future pending for review.

Daily plays, fun parlays and the 80/20 Climb keep their existing records. A months-long future must never make a
daily or weekly card look fuller, and open futures do not count as pending daily plays.

## Free rollout

1. Before each covered season, inventory which futures markets and exact prices are available from the feeds the
   desk already uses. Do not scrape a sportsbook or add a paid call.
2. Start an append-only paper ledger with original prices and dated movements. Save the preseason model snapshot so
   the eventual result can be evaluated honestly.
3. Show research watches separately by sport and season. Do not call a watch a play.
4. Publish an official future only after that sport has a defensible season model, complete settlement rules, a
   verified price and explicit owner approval for the new public card type.
5. At settlement, report the original price and result. Use closing-price movement only as context; it does not turn
   an unsettled future into a win.

Until those gates pass, the public Lab labels Season futures as planned. The first implementation work is the shared
append-only quote/settlement ledger; individual sport models and cards remain separate promotion decisions.
