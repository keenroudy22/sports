## Patterns

1. **Lottos lead.** The most saved posts are big-odds tickets (+1,000 to +16,000) with the odds in the first line, a
   betslip picture on a player or stadium photo, and a one-tap way to load it (a book link, or @Playbook). "One leg
   per game" and "every team at 1 PM" are the common shapes. People save them to tail.
2. **"Save this" sheets.** Cheat sheets of projections (Jimmy's anytime TD %, Toad's parlay pieces) get the most likes
   and saves among data posts, far more than the projection grid itself. The ask ("Save this post", "Bookmark this")
   is in the first line.
3. **Challenges that run for days.** Ladder ($50 to $1,000 in 7 bets), Roller ($1000 roll-over), Escalator (alt
   lines stepping up). Each step is a post, so people come back for the next one.
4. **A teaser, then the drop.** "Locked in. Want a look?", "Dropping shortly 👀", "❤️ & I'll drop it". The teaser
   often out-draws the pick.
5. **Receipts.** ✅ lists of hits with their odds, "we cashed" quoting the original with the winning slip. The
   pinned post is the biggest hit or the current lotto.
6. **Asks.** "❤️ and 🔁 if you're tailing", "Who's with me?", reply prompts.
7. **What flops.** Sportsbook sign-up promos, paid-VIP pushes and plain text picks without a picture: 3 to 20 likes.
8. **Units stay in play** (Caruso "+25 units, $100 bettors up $2500", Dan's ladder "1u / 0.5u / 0.2u", Fredo "3u").
9. **@Playbook** is tagged by Fredo too.

## Where we stand

We post a clean, consistent card for every play, a morning receipt and cashed posts, graded in public. We have what
the data accounts sell: a projection for every game and player, win probability and our chance on every line. What
we do not do yet: lotto framing, "save this" sheets, a running challenge, teasers, and asks.
