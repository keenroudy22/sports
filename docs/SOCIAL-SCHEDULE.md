# Kook'n publishing rhythm

This is the operating schedule followers can understand and the desk can actually keep. A release window is not a
promise that a play exists. Official plays still require a current line, a usable price and a clean news check.

## Game-day sequence (Eastern)

| Window | Purpose | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 8:45 AM | Today's menu | Only when already-approved plays exist. Never advertise a category that is not ready. |
| 9:00 AM | Receipt | Prior results, win or lose. Wednesday also includes the weekly receipt. |
| 10:00 AM | Saveable slate sheet | College Saturday and NFL Sunday. Research, not an official card. |
| 10:30 AM | Research / engagement | At most one evidence-first trend, matchup, injury or underdog graphic per football slate. |
| Around noon | Official plays | Earlier kickoffs post about two hours before kickoff. Pick of the Day first; ten minutes between posts. |
| Between the first two plays | Conversation | Only on a multi-play day. One short factual question or a real ready-ticket teaser. |
| 10:00 AM, 1:30 PM, 4:00 PM, 8:00 PM | 80/20 Climb scans | A rung posts only when two independent legs qualify. A settled rung may advance the same day. |
| After settlement | Cashed / Climb update | Wins can post within the approved window. Losses stay visible in the receipt. |
| 6:00 PM | Quiet-day record | Only when no more useful approved post supplied the daily presence. |

Confirmed plays and fun tickets reach Discord about 10–15 minutes before their X time. Other approved posts mirror
after X confirms delivery. `@Playbook` appears only on a new play on X. It never appears in Discord or on a result,
research, menu or engagement post.

## Engagement boundaries

- Useful and repeatable beats noisy: one slate sheet, one research card and one prompt are ceilings, not quotas.
- Results always follow the plays. A poor day does not pause honest posting and a good day does not hide the record.
- No automated replies, likes, follows, unfollows or trend chasing. Owner-written replies remain the safe way to join
  live conversations on X.
- Buffer is the only X publisher. Every public post keeps the daily cap and ten-minute spacing.
- Weekly decisions use like-for-like 24-hour and seven-day metrics after at least eight examples of a format.

## Data cadence behind the posts

- The Mac research desk checks football throughout the day and runs extra Climb scans four times daily.
- A 30-minute pre-post guard catches closed prices and hard news; Discord delivery checks every five minutes.
- The website's factual score views refresh directly from the public score source once a minute while open. Prices,
  forecasts and picks retain their captured timestamps and never pretend to be live.
- Monday at 9:30 AM the private weekly review measures operations, record, reach and timing and alerts the owner.
